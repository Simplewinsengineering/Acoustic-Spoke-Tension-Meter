User Guide & Physics Principles

1. How Acoustic Spoke Measurement Works

When plucked, a stretched bicycle spoke vibrates like a musical guitar string. The fundamental frequency depends directly on spoke length, diameter, density, and tension ($T = 4\mu L^2 f^2$).

2. Input Gain & Clarity Controls

Use the Input Gain Slider to boost quiet plucks or low-volume microphone inputs. Use the Clarity Threshold Slider to adjust pitch detection sensitivity: lower values detect weaker signals, while higher values suppress background ambient noise.

Measuring Free Vibration Length (L)

The free vibration length is the unconstrained section of the spoke that vibrates freely.

• Radial Spokes: Measure from hub flange to nipple edge.

• Crossed Spokes: Measure from the last contact point to the nipple edge.
